Tomohiro Motoda

元田 智大

Research Scientist, AIST (国立研究開発法人産業技術総合研究所), Odaiba, Tokyo, Japan

I am a research scientist in the Automation Research Team, Industrial CPS Research Center, AIST. My research topic is robotics, manipulation and machine learning (AI robotics). I received my Ph.D. in Engineering from Osaka University.
